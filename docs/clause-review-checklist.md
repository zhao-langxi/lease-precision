# Commercial retail lease · clause review checklist

**Status:** Generic checklist · not legal advice  
**Use:** First-pass structural read before counsel review or operator handoff

Run top to bottom. If a section is silent, note **"lease silent — risk"** rather than assuming landlord or tenant default.

## 1. Parties and premises

- [ ] Legal names of landlord entity and tenant entity (match state filings if possible)
- [ ] Premises address, suite/unit, approximate square footage
- [ ] Permitted use clause — exclusive or restricted? Continuous operation required?
- [ ] Delivery date vs. rent commencement date — who pays utilities in the gap?
- [ ] "As-is" acceptance language — what did tenant waive?

## 2. Term and options

- [ ] Initial term start/end dates (calendar, not vibes)
- [ ] Renewal options — how many, notice window, rent formula on renewal
- [ ] Early termination rights (either party) and associated fees
- [ ] Holdover rate if tenant stays past expiration
- [ ] Assignment and subletting — consent standard, profit sharing, recapture rights

## 3. Rent stack

- [ ] Base / fixed rent — amount, escalation schedule, abatement periods
- [ ] Additional rent definition — what's included, what's excluded
- [ ] CAM (common area maintenance) — pro-rata share formula, cap, audit rights, reconciliation timing
- [ ] Real estate taxes — pass-through method, contest rights, special assessments
- [ ] Insurance reimbursements — required coverages, landlord as additional insured
- [ ] Percentage rent (if any) — breakpoint, reporting, audit
- [ ] Late fees, interest rate, default interest compounding

## 4. Payment mechanics

- [ ] Due date, grace period, acceptable payment methods
- [ ] ACH / auto-debit authorization scope
- [ ] **Pay-without-setoff** clause — tenant must pay even during disputes?
- [ ] Security deposit — amount, use, return timeline, non-interest-bearing?

## 5. Utilities and services

- [ ] Who meters and pays: electric, gas, water, sewer, trash
- [ ] Shared-meter or allocated utilities (common in strip centers)
- [ ] Landlord-provided services vs. tenant responsibility

## 6. Maintenance and repairs

- [ ] HVAC — ownership, replacement threshold, PM contract requirements
- [ ] Roof, structure, parking lot, plumbing — landlord vs. tenant split
- [ ] Tenant repair obligations — interior only or more?
- [ ] Landlord entry rights — notice, hours, emergency
- [ ] Code compliance — who brings premises to code for tenant's use?

## 7. Alterations and signage

- [ ] Approval process for buildout, signage, exterior changes
- [ ] Removal / restoration obligations at end of term
- [ ] Landlord approval not to be unreasonably withheld — or silent?

## 8. Insurance and indemnity

- [ ] Required liability limits, property coverage, business interruption
- [ ] Mutual indemnity vs. one-sided tenant indemnity
- [ ] Waiver of subrogation
- [ ] Additional insured / certificate delivery deadlines

## 9. Default and remedies

- [ ] Monetary default — notice, cure period, acceleration
- [ ] Non-monetary default — cure periods, repeated default triggers
- [ ] Landlord remedies — lockout restrictions (state-dependent), termination, reletting, deficiency claims
- [ ] Tenant remedies if landlord defaults — abatement, termination, self-help limits

## 10. Guaranties and personal exposure

- [ ] Personal or corporate guaranty — scope, cap, burn-down, release conditions
- [ ] Cross-default with other landlord leases or affiliated entities
- [ ] SPI / financial reporting requirements on guarantor

## 11. Miscellaneous high-impact clauses

- [ ] Estoppel certificate obligations
- [ ] SNDA (subordination, non-disturbance, attornment)
- [ ] Force majeure — rent still due?
- [ ] Condemnation and casualty — termination vs. rebuild, rent abatement
- [ ] Exclusive use / co-tenancy (if applicable)
- [ ] Radius restrictions on tenant's other locations
- [ ] Permitted transfers on change of control

## 12. Exhibits and incorporated documents

- [ ] Site plan / exhibit A — does it match the space toured?
- [ ] Rules and regulations — incorporated by reference?
- [ ] Tenant improvement allowance — draw schedule, unused allowance forfeiture
- [ ] Bank ACH exhibit — account authorization scope

## Output template (after review)

```text
Lease summary — [internal codename only, no public client ID]

Term: [start] → [end]
Base rent: [schedule]
Additional rent triggers: [list]
Top 5 operator obligations: [plain language]
Top 5 landlord obligations: [plain language]
Calendar reminders: [renewal notice, CAM true-up, insurance cert, HVAC PM]
Counsel review needed on: [list ambiguous or aggressive clauses]
```

Keep summaries in private working folders until scrubbed for public patterns. **Do not commit operator-specific summaries to this repo.**
