## What transfers across rooms

| Pattern | Lease | ServeIT | Madrid |
|---------|-------|---------|--------|
| Assume insider knowledge | Legal cross-refs | Cascade chunk order | Spanish-only civic flows |
| Fix | Operator summary | Pre-ship checklist | Plain-language + `lang` markup |
| Handoff test | Can operator track rent/CAM? | Can partner update site? | Can outsider complete the form? |
| Slow progress | Checklist grows with deals | Docs update per sprint | Essays expand when patterns clarify |

## What stays separate

- **No private research or participant-data framing** anywhere in these public repos
- **No private lease PDFs** in lease-precision (unlike clinic repos that link to shipped public partner sites)
- **No legal advice** ... process only; counsel for binding calls
