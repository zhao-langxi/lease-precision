## Why this repo exists

Commercial leases are long, cross-referenced, and written for lawyers who already know the vocabulary. Small business tenants often sign without a plain-language map of what they owe, when, and what triggers default.

This repo collects **public-safe** checklists and documentation patterns for clause review, CAM and tax pass-throughs, estoppel and SNDA, personal guaranties, pay-without-setoff, and operator handoffs that include the landlord property manager. Employer omitted. Executed agreements and client materials stay local.

Same throughline as family restaurant work: a system either survives contact with a real operator or it doesn't.

## Principles

1. **Precision over polish** ... Name the clause, the trigger, and the dollar amount.
2. **Operator-readable** ... If the person running the shop can't follow the summary, rewrite it.
3. **No surprise dependencies** ... CAM, taxes, insurance, guarantees, and renewal options belong in one honest timeline.
4. **Public-safe only** ... Process and patterns here; private lease PDFs never.
